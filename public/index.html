How the overall score works

Everyone is ranked inside their own division (age group and sex). Overall ranks your sprint and jump together: for each test we look at how much of your division you beat, and average the two. You need both tests to get an overall place. Best of up to 3 trials counts for each test. The mile has its own board and doesn't count toward overall.
